ReviewArc opens to one queue of pull requests from the repositories you choose. Open a pull request to create an isolated copy, analyze the exact revision, and receive an evidence-backed recommendation with only the consequential judgments and unresolved uncertainty surfaced for you.

## What stays local

ReviewArc works in its own copy of the repository and does not modify your checkout. Analysis, progress, private notes, and drafts stay on your Mac until you publish a review.

[Read about security, local data, and publishing](/docs/security-and-local-data/).
